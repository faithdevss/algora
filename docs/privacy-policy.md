# AlgorAI — Privacy Policy

*Effective July 31, 2026*

AlgorAI ("the app") is a data structures, algorithms, and AI/ML learning app published by
Saimum Islam. This policy explains what data the app touches and why. If a category of data
isn't listed below, the app does not collect it.

## No account, no server

AlgorAI has no sign-up, no login, and no backend server. All of your progress — completed
topics, quiz history, flashcard review schedule, and settings — is stored only in local app
storage on your device (Android DataStore). It is never transmitted anywhere by the app.
Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android's own device backup is enabled, that local data
may be included in your normal device backup, same as any other app's — AlgorAI does not
manage or control that separately.

**In short:** nothing you do inside AlgorAI — which topics you study, how you score on a quiz,
which flashcards you review — leaves your device through anything AlgorAI itself writes.

## Permissions

`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — only for the optional daily study reminder, toggleable in Settings.
Scheduled locally on-device; nothing about it is sent anywhere.

## Third-party services

AlgorAI uses a small number of Google services to run rewarded ads, process the one-time
in-app purchase, and show the native Play "rate this app" prompt. Each is Google's own SDK
under Google's own terms — AlgorAI adds no tracking of its own on top.

- **Google AdMob** — ads, in two places and nowhere else. **Rewarded video** is optional: watch one
  to unlock a locked topic for 6 hours, or to bank a streak freeze. It is only ever shown if you tap
  "watch an ad". **A full-screen ad** may also appear when you leave the results of a completed quiz
  — at most three a day, never during a quiz, and never for anyone who has bought Premium. Google's
  ad SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, per
  [Google's ad policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Google Play Billing** — handles the one lifetime "remove ads / unlock everything" purchase.
  Payment is processed entirely by Google Play. AlgorAI never sees or stores card details —
  only Play's confirmation the purchase was made.
- **Google Play In-App Review** — the native "rate AlgorAI" prompt shown occasionally after
  positive usage signals. Handled entirely by Play's review API; AlgorAI doesn't see your
  rating or review text.

## What AlgorAI does not do

- No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.
- No user accounts, sign-in, or personal profile of any kind.
- No chat, comments, or any way for users to share content with each other.
- No sale of data — there is no data collected by AlgorAI itself to sell.

## Children

AlgorAI is listed for a general audience (13+) and is not directed at children. It does not
knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
